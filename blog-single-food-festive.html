Food Festive 2025

A true celebration of taste and culture, our annual Food Festive brings you an incredible array of local delicacies and international flavors. From traditional mountain dishes to gourmet fusion, it's a day dedicated to the joy of cooking and the rich culinary heritage of our region.

This year, we are featuring over 50 local chefs who will be showcasing their signature dishes. Enjoy everything from authentic Nepali dal bhat and momos to artisanal cheeses, freshly baked breads, and exotic international cuisines.

The festival also includes live cooking demonstrations, wine tasting sessions, and interactive workshops where you can learn the secrets behind some of the most famous local recipes.

Join us at The Hotel for a gastronomic journey. Special culinary packages are available for our guests, including exclusive tasting menus and private dining experiences.
