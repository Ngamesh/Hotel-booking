World Rap Day with UNIQ POET

Celebrate the rhythm and energy of Pokhara during World Rap Day. This event brings together the best local and national rap artists for an unforgettable night of music, art, and vibrant energy right in the heart of Lakeside. Join us for a showcase of talent that defines the modern music scene of Nepal.

Uniq Poet, one of the most celebrated artists in the Nepali hip-hop scene, will be headlining the event. His dynamic stage presence and deep lyrical meaning have resonated with thousands of fans.

The event is not just about music; it's a celebration of street culture, unity, and expression. With live graffiti art, breakdance battles, and pop-up food stalls, the entire area transforms into a massive creative festival.

Book your stay with us and get VIP access to the event. Our hotel is just a few steps away from the main stage, giving you the perfect retreat after an energetic night out.
